How it works

Fair chores in three simple steps

Set up your household in under a minute and let the weekly schedule run itself.

01

Create your household

Sign up, name your home, and add everyone who lives there—even roommates or family members without login accounts.

02

Add chores & rotation order

List recurring weekly tasks like taking out the trash or cleaning the kitchen, then set the exact order members rotate through.
